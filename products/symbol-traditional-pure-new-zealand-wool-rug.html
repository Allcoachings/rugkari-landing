Common Questions

About the Symbol Traditional

What size should I order for my living room?
For Indian living rooms: a 6x9 ft works for front-legs-on-rug placement with a 3-seater sofa (the most common Indian layout). An 8x10 ft covers full-furniture-on-rug with a 3+2 setup. For drawing rooms or open plan, go 9x12 ft or larger. Custom sizing is available — message us on WhatsApp.
Is this rug genuinely hand-made?
Yes. Every Rugkari rug is hand-crafted by a single master artisan in our Bhadohi atelier — not machine-tufted in a factory. The Symbol Traditional takes 14– 18 days of continuous work to complete. You can request video proof of weaving before dispatch on commissioned pieces.
Why is Rugkari more affordable than design stores?
Rugkari is the maker, not a reseller. There's no design studio markup, no import margin, no retailer cut. You pay for the rug, the wool, and the artisan's time — direct from Bhadohi. The same heirloom-quality piece costs 3– 4x more at gallery design stores.
How do I care for a pure NZ wool rug?
Vacuum weekly with suction-only (no beater bar). Rotate 180 degrees every 6 months for even wear. Blot spills with cold water immediately — never hot. Professional wool cleaning every 18– 24 months for high-traffic rooms. See our complete wool care guide.
What is covered under the 25-year warranty?
Manufacturing defects: loose weaving, unravelling edges, significant colour bleeding, pattern deviation, or size discrepancy over 2%. Not covered: normal wear, sun fading, pet/water damage, or harsh chemical use. See our full warranty details.
What if it doesn't look right in my room?
7-day returns from delivery for manufacturing defects. For change-of-mind on standard sizes, free pickup is available within 48 hours — 5% admin fee applies. Custom orders are non-returnable. See our full refund policy.
