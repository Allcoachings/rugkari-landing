Buying Guide

The Best Rugs for Bedrooms in India (2026 Buyer Guide)

By Rugkari Editorial Team2026-05-11Buying Guide

A bedroom rug does three things: it warms the floor (especially in cool months), it absorbs sound (making the bedroom quieter), and it adds the soft visual contrast a bedroom needs. Here is exactly how to choose one.

Three ways to place a bedroom rug

The standard placement options:

  1. Full under bed: the rug extends 18 to 24 inches beyond all three sides (left, right, foot of bed). The headboard sits on bare floor or against the wall. Requires the largest rug.
  2. Two-thirds under bed: the rug starts roughly under the pillows and extends past the foot of the bed. Bedside tables sit on bare floor. Mid-cost option.
  3. Runners on each side: two long narrow rugs (2x6 ft each) flank the bed. No rug under the bed itself. Most affordable, works in small bedrooms.

Material: low-pile or high-pile for bedrooms?

Bedroom traffic is light (you walk on the rug only when getting in and out of bed), so pile depth is a comfort choice, not a durability one.

Low-pile (8 to 12mm) like the Rugkari Mist Abstract is best for bedrooms with cool flooring (marble, vitrified tiles) where you want softness underfoot but easy vacuuming.

High-pile (20mm hand-tufted) like the Rugkari Elysian Abstract is best for bedrooms with wooden or warm flooring where you want plush, foot-sinking softness on bare feet in the morning.

Bedroom rug color and pattern

Bedrooms need calm. Two design principles:

1. The rug should be lighter or softer in color than the most dominant element in the room. If you have a strong headboard, a busy duvet, or bold curtains, the rug should be the quietest element.

2. If your bedroom uses muted neutrals, the rug can be the visual interest. Abstract patterns and subtle geometrics work well here. Avoid loud florals or strong stripes which can feel restless at night.

Frequently Asked

Common Questions

What size rug for a queen bed?
For a queen bed (5x6.5 ft), an 8x10 ft rug works for full-under-bed placement, 6x9 ft for two-thirds placement, or two 2x6 ft side runners.
What size rug for a king bed?
For a king bed (6x6.5 ft), use a 9x12 ft rug for full-under-bed placement, 8x10 ft for two-thirds, or two 2.5x6 ft side runners.
Should the bedside tables sit on the rug?
In full-under-bed placement, yes (the rug should extend at least 18 inches beyond the bedside tables). In two-thirds placement, the bedside tables sit on bare floor.
Are wool rugs good for bedrooms?
Yes, especially pure New Zealand wool. Wool is naturally hypoallergenic, has built-in stain resistance from lanolin, is flame-retardant, and feels luxurious on bare feet. For Indian climate, wool rugs feel cool in summer and warm in winter.
What is the most popular bedroom rug size in India?
6x9 ft for queen beds and 8x10 ft for king beds, both in two-thirds-under-bed placement, are the most popular configurations for Indian master bedrooms.
