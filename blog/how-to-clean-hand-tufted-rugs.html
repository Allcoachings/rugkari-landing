How to Clean Hand-Tufted Wool Rugs at Home: Step-by-Step (2026)

By Rugkari Editorial TeamPublished 2026-05-16 · Updated 2026-05-16Care Guide

Cleaning a hand-tufted wool rug at home takes 20 minutes a week and prevents 90% of long-term damage. The rules are simple: vacuum on suction-only mode, blot spills (never rub), rotate every six months and book a professional cold-water wash every 18 to 24 months. Below is the complete protocol used by master artisans in Bhadohi — what to do, when, and the four things that quietly destroy hand-tufted rugs.

What is a hand-tufted rug, and why is its care different?

A hand-tufted rug is woven by tufting yarn through a primary canvas with a pneumatic tufting gun, then sealing the reverse with natural latex and a secondary backing. This construction is plush and affordable, but the latex layer needs the rug to stay dry on the reverse — which is why steam-cleaning, soaking and machine-washing are off-limits. Care that works for a flat-weave dhurrie or a hand-knotted rug can ruin a hand-tufted piece.

The weekly routine — vacuum on suction only

Run the vacuum once a week. The single rule: switch off or lift the beater bar. The motorised brush is designed for short, synthetic carpet — on a 20mm wool pile it grabs the loops and accelerates shedding by roughly 3x. Slow, overlapping strokes in the direction of the pile, suction set to medium, one minute per square metre. That is the entire weekly routine.

The monthly routine

  • Lift the corners and shake outdoors to release trapped grit at the base of the pile.
  • Check the underlay for slipping — adjust if the rug has shifted by more than 5 cm.
  • Inspect the binding along the edges. If you see a thread starting to unravel, send a photo to Rugkari via WhatsApp — most binding repairs are warranty-covered.

The six-month routine

Rotate the rug 180°. This is the single most important habit for long-term life — it equalises foot-traffic wear and sun fading across the full surface. Rugs left in one orientation for years show a visible "high-traffic stripe" along the main walking path that no amount of cleaning will reverse.

Spill response — the first 60 seconds matter most

Pure New Zealand wool has a natural lanolin coating that resists liquid for roughly 30 to 60 seconds before absorption begins. Use that window.

  1. Blot, never rub. Press a dry white cotton cloth onto the spill from the outside of the stain inward. Apply firm pressure for 10 seconds, lift, refold to a dry section, blot again. Repeat until the cloth comes up dry.
  2. If pigment remains, mist a 1:20 solution of pH-neutral wool detergent in cold water onto the affected area. Blot dry again.
  3. If a hard residue forms, gently lift it with the back of a butter knife once dry — do not pull at the pile.
  4. Never use hot water. Heat sets protein-based stains (coffee, tea, wine, blood) permanently into wool.

For specific stain protocols, see our coffee-stain removal guide.

Four things to never do to a hand-tufted rug

1. Never steam-clean. The heat melts the latex backing and felts the wool pile.

2. Never soak. Saturating the rug breaks the latex set and causes the secondary backing to delaminate from the primary canvas.

3. Never machine-wash. The spin cycle felts wool permanently. The weight will damage most residential machines.

4. Never use bleach, ammonia or carpet shampoo. These strip wool's lanolin and dull the dye irreversibly.

Professional deep cleaning — what to expect

Every 18 to 24 months, schedule a professional cold-water wash. A reputable service collects the rug, immerses it in a flat-bed cold-water tank with pH-neutral wool soap, soft-brushes the pile in the direction of weave, rinses thoroughly, dewaters between rollers and air-dries flat over 24 to 48 hours.

Indicative cost in India: Rs. 25 to Rs. 60 per square foot. A 6x9 ft hand-tufted rug runs Rs. 1,800 to Rs. 3,600. Rugkari customers receive a complimentary first professional wash within the warranty period — message us on WhatsApp to book.

Tools to keep at home

You do not need specialist equipment. The full kit:

  • A vacuum with adjustable suction and a beater-bar off switch.
  • Six clean white cotton cloths (kept in a basket near the rug).
  • One bottle of pH-neutral wool detergent — Eucalan, Soak, or similar.
  • One spray bottle for cold water.
  • A soft-bristle brush for occasional pile fluffing.

Frequently Asked

Hand-Tufted Rug Cleaning FAQs

How often should I clean a hand-tufted wool rug?
Vacuum once a week with the beater bar off. Spot-clean spills immediately. Rotate the rug every six months. Schedule a professional cold-water wash every 18 to 24 months. This routine extends the life of a hand-tufted pure New Zealand wool rug to 12 to 15 years and beyond.
Can I steam-clean a hand-tufted rug?
No. Steam permanently mats wool pile and breaks the latex backing on hand-tufted rugs. Use cold water and pH-neutral wool detergent only. Steam cleaning voids the manufacturer warranty on every hand-tufted rug sold in India.
What is the safest stain remover for wool rugs?
Cold water and a few drops of pH-neutral wool detergent in a spray bottle is the safest universal cleaner. Avoid bleach, ammonia, hydrogen peroxide and any product labelled for synthetic carpet — these strip the lanolin coating on wool and dull the colour.
How do I stop a new hand-tufted rug from shedding?
You do not stop it — you wait it out. All new hand-tufted wool rugs shed loose fibres for the first 8 to 12 weeks. Vacuum gently on suction-only mode for the first month and the shedding resolves on its own.
Can I wash a hand-tufted rug in a machine?
No. The latex backing and 4,000+ gsm wool weight will damage residential and commercial washing machines, and the spin cycle felts and shrinks the wool. Only professional rug-washing services with flat-floor cold-water systems are safe.
How much does professional rug cleaning cost in India?
Professional cold-water wool rug cleaning in India typically costs Rs. 25 to Rs. 60 per square foot. A 6x9 ft hand-tufted rug usually costs Rs. 1,800 to Rs. 3,600 to wash. Rugkari customers receive a complimentary first wash within the warranty period.
