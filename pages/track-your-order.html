Order Care

Know where your rug is

Once your order ships, a tracking link is sent to you via WhatsApp and email. If you can't find it, you can also check your order status by contacting our team directly — we respond within working hours.

Three Ways To Track

Pick what's easiest

01 · Check WhatsApp / Email

Your order confirmation and tracking ID are sent via WhatsApp and email the moment your rug ships. Search your inbox for "Rugkari" or check your WhatsApp message thread with our team.

Common Questions

Quick answers

My order hasn't shipped yet — what should I do?
In-stock orders typically dispatch within 1– 2 working days. Custom and made-to-order rugs follow the production timeline (10– 35 working days depending on construction). If it has been longer than expected, WhatsApp our team with your order number and we'll check on it.
My package looks tampered with — what do I do?
Do not accept delivery. Refuse the package at the door and immediately WhatsApp or email us with photographs. We will arrange a replacement at no cost.
I need urgent help with my order. How do I reach you?
WhatsApp +91 73485 15188 for fastest response (Mon– Sat, 10:00– 18:00 IST). For non-urgent matters, email care@rugkari.com.
