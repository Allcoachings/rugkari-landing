About the Collection

Why Rugkari Solid Rugs

Solid rugs are the foundation of layered interiors. They ground furniture without competing, and the unbroken surface lets the texture of pure New Zealand wool become the visual story.

Rugkari solid rugs use single-tone or close-tonal palettes with no synthetic dye contrast. 20mm pile depth, hand-pushed through cotton canvas, woven over 14-18 days in Bhadohi.
