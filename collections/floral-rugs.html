About the Collection

Why Rugkari Floral Rugs

Floral design in rugs has come a long way from the heavy Victorian carpets of the 20th century. Rugkari floral rugs use softer, larger botanical motifs that complement contemporary furniture rather than overwhelm it.

Each floral rug is hand-crafted in pure New Zealand wool, with botanical patterns inspired by Indian gardens and traditional Mughal motifs reinterpreted for modern interiors.
