About the Collection

Why Rugkari Bedroom Rugs

A bedroom rug is sized to extend at least 60cm past the sides and foot of the bed so you step onto wool, not bare floor. Calmer pattern languages $EM solids, soft abstracts, gentle florals $EM keep the room restful.

Rugkari bedroom rugs use 20mm pile pure New Zealand wool, hand-tufted in Bhadohi. The pile compresses gently underfoot and rebounds. Backed by our heirloom warranty.
