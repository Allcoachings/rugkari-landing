About the Collection

Why Rugkari Geometric Rugs

Geometric design is the most flexible pattern language in rugs. It works under modern furniture, traditional furniture and everything in between. Scale is the variable that decides the mood: tight repeats for energy, large blocks for calm.

Each Rugkari geometric rug is handcrafted in pure New Zealand wool with a 20mm pile. Patterns are carved with hand-finishing for clean edges and tonal depth.
