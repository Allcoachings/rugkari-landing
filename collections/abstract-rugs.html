About the Collection

Why Rugkari Abstract Rugs

Abstract design in a rug is a balancing act. Too loud and it competes with the furniture; too muted and it disappears. Rugkari abstract rugs are tuned for Indian living rooms where seating volumes are large, light is warm, and the rug needs to ground without overpowering.

Each Rugkari abstract piece is hand-crafted in pure New Zealand wool with a 20mm ultra-luxury pile. No synthetic dyes, no shortcuts. Backed by our 10-year heirloom warranty.
