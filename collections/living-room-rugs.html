About the Collection

Why Rugkari Living Room Rugs

A living room rug should fit under at least the front legs of the main sofa, with breathing space at the centre table. In Indian homes that often means 8x10 or 9x12; oversized for studios and larger for open-plan layouts.

Rugkari living room rugs use 20mm pile pure New Zealand wool, hand-tufted or hand-knotted in Bhadohi. Pattern language spans abstract, geometric, designer and traditional. Backed by our heirloom warranty.
