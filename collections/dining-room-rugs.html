About the Collection

Why Rugkari Dining Room Rugs

A dining rug is sized to the table, not the room. The rule of thumb: 60cm of rug clearance on every side beyond the table edge so chairs stay on the rug when pulled out. Pattern should read top-down, not just side-on.

Rugkari dining rugs are handcrafted in pure New Zealand wool with 20mm pile, structured pattern language, and edges hand-finished for daily traffic. Spill-cleaning is straightforward with our care kit.
