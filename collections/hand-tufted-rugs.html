About the Collection

Why Rugkari Hand-Tufted Rugs

Hand-tufted rugs are the most popular choice for modern Indian homes. They have the genuine handmade character of hand-knotted rugs at a fraction of the cost, with a denser, plusher pile that feels luxurious underfoot.

Every Rugkari hand-tufted rug uses 100% pure New Zealand wool with a 20mm ultra-luxury pile depth, cotton canvas backing, and is woven by a single artisan over 14-18 days in Bhadohi.
